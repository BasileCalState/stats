Partial vs Semi-Partial Correlation

Understanding correlations in sports performance research

The Research Question

Scenario:

Does weekly training hours predict athletic performance scores? But we know that experience (years competing) affects both training capacity and performance. How do we isolate the true relationship?

Key Takeaways

1. Magnitude Relationship

Simple ≥ Partial ≥ Semi-Partial

This order always holds (in absolute value)

2. When to Use Partial

Research Question: "Do these two variables relate independently of a third factor?"

Example: Does anxiety relate to performance, independent of skill level?

3. When to Use Semi-Partial

Research Question: "How much unique variance does this predictor add?"

Example: Do mental skills training predict performance beyond physical training?

Practical Note:

Semi-partial correlations are what you're actually looking at in multiple regression when you examine individual predictors. Each beta coefficient represents the unique contribution of that variable, which is based on semi-partial (not partial) correlation.
